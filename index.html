Elena Boni

I am a Ph.D student in Economics at ENS Lyon and affiliated to the Center for Economic Research on Governance, Inequality and Conflict (CERGIC), under the supervision of Sylvie Démurger and Dionissi Aliprantis.

My main research interests include the economics of migration and education, with particular focus on the role of international students. I am also interested in the impact of climate change on gender outcomes.

I hold a Master’s degree from LMU Munich. Before joining CERGIC, I worked as a Research Assistant at the ifo Center for Energy, Climate, and Resources, and I was a Predoc at LEAP Bocconi. More details on my updated CV.
